Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChasePay a payment processor like Stripe?

+

No, ChasePay is a workflow and automation tool. We do not process payments directly. Instead, we help you generate invoices and automate the follow-up process, instructing clients to pay via your preferred method (bank transfer, Stripe link, PayPal, etc.).

How do the AI reminders work?

+

Our engine monitors invoice due dates. Based on the lateness of the payment, it generates a polite, contextual email. Early reminders are gentle nudges; later ones are firmer. You can always review or pause them.

Will emails come from my own address?

+

Yes! If you connect your Gmail or use custom SMTP on the Agency plan, emails are sent natively from your address, maintaining your professional relationship with the client.
